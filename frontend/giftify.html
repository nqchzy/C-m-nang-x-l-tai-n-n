Can I use a low-resolution photo?
We recommend photos at least 1000×1000px for best results. Our upload tool will warn you if resolution is too low.

Is the print dishwasher safe?
Yes! All our mugs use sublimation printing which is fully dishwasher and microwave safe.

Can I return a personalized item?
If there's a defect or print error on our side, we'll remake it for free. We don't accept returns for personalization changes, so review your preview carefully.
